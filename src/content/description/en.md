He was admitted to the Portuguese Bar in 2024. His practice covers corporate taxation in M&A and restructurings, tax litigation and the international taxation of individuals and companies.

He previously worked as a corporate tax consultant at KPMG Portugal (2024–2025) and completed his Bar traineeship in the Tax department of Abreu Advogados (2022–2024).

He holds a law degree from the University of Coimbra, which included an Erasmus+ exchange at KU Leuven, Belgium, and a Postgraduate Diploma in Tax Law from IDEFF, Faculty of Law of the University of Lisbon. {dissertation}

He is a junior researcher at the Nova Tax Research Lab. He was also a junior researcher at the Instituto Brasileiro de Direito Tributário (IBDT) and, during his time at KU Leuven, a research assistant at the Institute of Social Law. He writes on international taxation, the taxation of the digital economy and crypto-assets, and EU tax law. His work has appeared in the Nova Tax Research Series, the Nova Tax Practitioners Column and CMS LawNow.
